# The Rooster: pitch material (private, not published)

The Rooster is a restaurant in Riga Old Town, near Dome Square. It has a new stand at the Christmas market. The owner is Sandis, who also owns The Crust (@the.crust.riga). His wife runs The Crust's Instagram. The Rooster has no Instagram or TikTok yet. Shaun, our agent, introduced him on 25 Nov 2026.

The plan is the same idea as Grilvista's, adjusted for tourists: English first, Old Town and Christmas-market formats, Google and TripAdvisor reviews, and cross-promotion with The Crust. There is no price in it; price is discussed at the meeting.

## Files

- `plan/plan.html` (variant A) and `plan/plan-b.html` (variant B: photo cards, a timeline, a numbers band): one-page A4 plans with the same content. LV by default, `?lang=en` for English. `node marketing/rooster/plan/export.mjs` writes `rooster-plans-lv.pdf`, `rooster-plans-en.pdf` and previews. The fonts are shared with `marketing/grilvista/assets/fonts`.
- `plan/frame-1..3.jpg`: stills from Shaun's video (the storefront, the Christmas window, the market stand).
