## How leads are scored (out of 100)

The ECC lead-intelligence weights are for investors and founders on X and LinkedIn. These are adapted for local walk-in businesses:

| Signal | Weight | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| No site, or a weak one | 35 | Only Facebook or Instagram, a broken site, or a site that doesn't work on a phone |
| Visual business | 20 | Food, hair, nails, cakes: photos sell it |
| Already posts on Facebook/Instagram | 15 | They care about marketing and already have content to use |
| Needs bookings or orders | 15 | Salon, barber, pizza or cake orders: online booking is a concrete reason to pay |
| Walk-in, central, easy to visit | 10 | You can drop in and show the demo on a phone |
| Reviews and activity | 5 | A busy place with reviews has money and something to show |

## Outreach

The best channel for a local business is **walking in with the demo open on a phone**. After that comes an Instagram or Facebook message, then a call. Email is the weakest. Never send anything in bulk, and never with the same text.

### In person (Tier 1)

> Labdien! Esmu Kārlis, mēs ar draugu Gustavu taisām mājaslapas Jelgavas uzņēmumiem. Uztaisījām jums paraugu, lai redzat, kā tas izskatītos. Drīkst parādīt? Tas aizņems minūti.
>
> *(Show it on the phone: first screen, then the one thing built only for them.)*
>
> Šobrīd, kad kāds Google meklē "[keratīns / kafejnīca / picas] Jelgava", jūs neparādāties. Ar šo lapu un sakārtotu Google profilu parādītos. Ja patīk, varam sarunāt cenu, ja ne, paldies, ka paskatījāties.

One ask only: "Drīkst parādīt?" Leave the price for the second step.

### Instagram/Facebook DM (SKY BEAUTY example)

> Labdien! Redzējām jūsu video "Sausi, pūkaini un bojāti mati?" un uztaisījām SKY BEAUTY mājaslapas paraugu ar jūsu pirms/pēc bildēm, rudens kodu RUDENS un pierakstu tiešsaistē. Varam atsūtīt 1 minūtes video, kā tas izskatās?

### Follow-up after 4–5 days, once only

> Sveiki vēlreiz, vai paspējāt paskatīties? Ja ne tagad, nekas, paraugs paliek, ja kādreiz noderēs.

### For tier 2, before a demo exists

Don't build a full demo for a cold lead. Send one screen or a 20-second video first. Build the whole site only if they reply.
